Primary Compliance Pillar

POSH Act 2013 — Zero-Tolerance Governance

A legal mandate for every Indian employer. Protect your firm, directors, and culture.

⚖️ Section 4 Mandates

Every office or administrative unit with 10 or more employees must constitute an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC). The ICC must have a Presiding Officer (senior woman employee) and at least one external independent legal member.

💥 Statutory Penalties: Failure to constitute the ICC or file the Annual Compliance Report results in a ₹50,000 fine. Repeated violations lead to a double fine and permanent cancellation of the business license.

🧙‍♂️ How AA Plus Automates It

  • The POSH Committee Wizard: HR enters ICC names, photos, and emails once. The platform dynamically injects them into the final slide of all learner modules, proving statutory notice.
  • Auto-Generated POSH Policy: Instantly generates a customized, legally compliant corporate POSH Policy PDF for board signature and distribution.
  • Mandatory 100% Completion tracking: Tracks and locks completions. Generates verified employee registry logs ready for stock exchange audit filings (SEBI BRSR).
Data & Threat Security

DPDP Act 2023 & CERT-In Safeguards

India's strict personal data and security disclosure policies. Protect client trust and avoid massive liabilities.

🔒 MeitY & DPDP 2023 Frameworks

India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023** holds corporations fully accountable as "Data Fiduciaries." Businesses must train all employees handling personal identifiers on data minimization, secure processing, and consent boundaries.

💥 Severe Financial Liabilities: Statutorily defines corporate fines up to ₹250 Crore per breach for failing to take "reasonable security safeguards" to prevent personal data exposure.

⏰ The CERT-In 6-Hour Emergency

Under MeitY directives, all cybersecurity incidents (phishing, ransomware, leakages) must be reported to **CERT-In within 6 hours** of discovery.

  • Incident Hygiene Training: Pre-built CERT-In compliance modules instruct teams on immediate reporting and phishing defense.
  • Underwriter Auditing: Delivers standard compliance certificates required by insurers to activate cyber-liability policy payouts.
Corporate Integrity

Board Fiduciary & SEBI LODR Vetting

Aligning management, independent directors, and KMPs against Section 166, 177, and 188 mandates.

♟️ Section 188 Related Party Transactions

RPTs are the highest source of regulatory auditing failures in India. Under Section 188 of the Companies Act, RPTs that are not at arm's length or in the ordinary course must obtain formal Board or Shareholder approval.

  • Section 177: Audit Committee holds absolute prior vetting authority.
  • Board voting: Conflicted directors are statutorily barred from the room.
  • RPT Interactive Simulator: Aligns management, finance, and procurement on correct SEBI materiality disclosures (10% of annual turnover).

🚨 KMP Personal Liability & SEBI Rules

Under the Companies Act, CFOs, CEOs, and Company Secretaries are labeled **"Officers in Default"** and face personal civil/criminal liabilities for compliance lapses.

📢 SEBI LODR Regulation 30: Listed companies must disclose material Board resolutions (fundraising, acquisitions, dividend declarations) to the stock exchange within **30 minutes** of board closure.
