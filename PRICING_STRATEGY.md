# AA Plus Policy Training Platform — Commercial Pricing Strategy

> Prepared: May 2026 | Based on competitive benchmarking of 15+ LMS/compliance platforms

---

## 1. Product Positioning

This is **not** a generic LMS — it is a **India-native compliance content + delivery platform** with:

- 11+ pre-built regulatory modules (POSH, DPDP Act 2023, CERT-In, Anti-Corruption, etc.)
- Auto-generated certificates with QR verification
- Multi-tenant B2B architecture (orgs, departments, roles)
- Audit trails and Excel reporting
- AI Policy tiers, RPT Simulator, POSH wizard
- Automated nudges and deadline tracking

Closest global comparisons: **Ethena** ($20–50/user/year, US-focused) and **Skillcast** (UK compliance-specialist).
Closest India comparisons: **POSH Check** (₹30,000–55,000/year, POSH only) and **Disprz** (~$3/user/month, full LMS).

**Competitive gap:** No India-native platform combines transparent pricing + bundled compliance content (POSH + Data Privacy + Cybersecurity + AI Policy) + verifiable certificates at an accessible price point.

---

## 2. Recommended Pricing Model

### Primary Model: **Annual Subscription by Employee Band**

**Why this model:**
- Indian HR/Finance teams strongly prefer predictable annual costs (no "active user" surprises)
- Compliance training is mandatory for all employees — not just active learners
- Simplest to quote, invoice (GST-compliant INR), and renew
- Matches how POSH Check and TalentLMS win Indian SMBs

**Currency:** Publish natively in **INR** with 18% GST. Show USD for MNCs. Accept via Razorpay (UPI AutoPay, e-NACH, cards).

## 8. Go-to-Market Packaging Recommendations

### Package Naming (customer-facing)
Avoid technical names. Recommended names:

| Internal | Customer-Facing Name |
|---|---|
| Starter | **Essentials** |
| Growth | **Business** |
| Scale | **Professional** |
| Professional | **Corporate** |
| Enterprise | **Enterprise** |

### Anchor Pricing Strategy
- Lead with **Professional (₹1,40,000/year)** as the "anchor" in sales conversations
- Most SMBs will land on **Business (₹42,000)** or **Professional** — the anchor makes Business feel affordable
- Show per-employee-per-day cost: ₹42,000 ÷ 100 ÷ 365 = **₹1.15/employee/day** — very compelling framing

### Annual vs Monthly Billing
- Default to annual billing (better cash flow, lower churn)
- Offer monthly billing at **+20% premium** for orgs under 100 employees only
- Do not offer monthly for Enterprise

## 9. What to Build to Support Monetization

To implement this pricing model, the following platform features are needed:

### Must-Have (Pre-Launch)
- [ ] **Plan/tier gating in DB**: `organizations.plan_tier` column (starter/growth/scale/professional/enterprise)
- [ ] **Feature flags by tier**: Module add-ons, admin seat limits, report export, webhooks — gated by tier
- [ ] **Razorpay subscription integration**: UPI AutoPay / e-NACH for annual recurring billing
- [ ] **GST-compliant invoice generation**: INR invoice with GSTIN, HSN code for SaaS (998314)
- [ ] **Plan upgrade/downgrade flow**: Self-serve upgrade prompt when hitting seat/feature limits
- [ ] **Trial period logic**: 14-day full-access trial with auto-expire and upgrade prompt

### Nice-to-Have (Post-Launch)
- [ ] **Usage dashboard for admins**: Show seat usage vs. plan limit
- [ ] **Renewal reminder emails**: 30/14/7 days before expiry
- [ ] **Annual report card**: Send org admin a PDF compliance summary at year-end (drives renewal)
- [ ] **Referral/partner program**: CA firms, law firms, HR consultants can refer clients for 15–20% commission

## 11. Key Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Price too high vs POSH Check (₹30K) | Emphasize 11 modules vs 1; POSH-only add-on at ₹25K competes directly |
| Price too low, undervalues product | "Per employee per day" framing + anchor pricing at Corporate tier |
| Enterprises want custom content | Enterprise tier includes custom module slot add-on |
| Monthly billing complexity | Restrict monthly to <100 employee plans only at +20% premium |
| Indian HRMS integration required | Roadmap item; use Zapier/webhook workaround in interim |

## Summary: Recommended Immediate Action

1. **Launch with 4 tiers** (Essentials / Business / Professional / Corporate) + Enterprise custom
2. **POSH-Only add-on at ₹25,000** as a standalone entry product to compete with POSH Check
3. **14-day free trial** (no credit card) to reduce friction
4. **INR pricing with Razorpay** — GST invoices mandatory for B2B India
5. **Publish pricing publicly** on the landing page — transparency wins trust with Indian SMBs and avoids the "sales call just to get a price" friction
